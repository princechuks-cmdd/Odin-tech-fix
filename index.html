Protect your device and data

Safety comes first

Stop if there is a hazard

If a device is swollen, smoking, sparking, unusually hot, wet while powered or smells burnt, stop using it. Do not charge or open it; seek qualified help.

Protect your data

Back up important files before resets or repairs. Factory resets, formatting and reinstalling systems can erase data.

Use official sources

Download updates and drivers from official sources. Never share passwords or one-time codes with someone offering support.

Know the limits

Odin provides general guidance, not a physical inspection or guaranteed diagnosis. Stop if a step risks hardware or data.
